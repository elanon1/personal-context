# Identity Onboarding — fill order & priority

> The vault ships empty. This is the order to populate it, highest value-per-effort first.
> The agent runs this as a guided interview when you're ready. Tackle one phase at a time.

## Phase 1 — Core identity (do first, ~15 min)
Highest payoff. After this, the agent can draft in your voice and stay on-target.
- [ ] `profile.md`: name, role at Volt, location/timezone, languages.
- [ ] `profile.md`: **writing voice & tone** ← the most reused signal. Be specific.
- [ ] `profile.md`: hard preferences and dislikes.
- [ ] `NOW.md`: top 3 goals + list of active projects.

## Phase 2 — Goals & tooling
- [ ] `goals.md`: short / medium / long-term goals with rough horizons.
- [ ] `tools-stack.md`: primary tools, systems, dev environment.

## Phase 3 — People
- [ ] `People/`: seed the 5–10 people you interact with most (use `_Templates/person.md`).

## Phase 4 — Ongoing migration (no end state)
- [ ] Create `10_Projects/<slug>/_state.md` as projects come up.
- [ ] Capture knowledge into `20_Knowledge/` as it arises.
- [ ] Let `_Inbox/` absorb everything else; process it during periodic summaries.
